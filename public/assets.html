Your role as site / office asset manager — asset management is control, not just an inventory
  • Validate what the probe discovers — auto-discovery only detects a device; move it out of Discovered into In Store / In Use once you confirm the site, owner, cost and tag.
  • Tag everything — no asset should reach In Store or In Use without an asset tag and QR code (the bridge between the register and the field).
  • Complete the record — fill the owner (user & department), acquisition date and purchase cost. A blank record is detected, not managed.
  • Keep it audited — run the quarterly audit and clear Never scanned and Ghost (healthy but stale > 90 days) devices; document the gaps and corrective actions.
  • Every movement leaves proof — receive, deploy, repair, loan, dispose: record the evidence (vendor, user, fault, data-wipe certificate…) at each step.
Golden rule — for every asset the register must answer: what · where · who · what state · since when · what cost · with what proof. This dashboard measures exactly that.

Par unité d'organisation — où rattacher la GPO

On rattache une GPO à une OU, pas à un site : un site couvre plusieurs OU. Le total AD du conteneur accompagne le nombre d'écarts — 2 sur 2 et 2 sur 400 n'appellent pas la même action.
