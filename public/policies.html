Every regulation rule the platform applies, with its default. Set a value for the whole platform or for one site; a site value wins over the platform value. Relaxing a protected rule needs a super_admin and a written reason, and is logged as a risk. Every change is kept in the history.

Assouplir une règle protégée exige un super_admin et un motif écrit, journalisé comme risque. Chaque changement est conservé dans l'historique.
