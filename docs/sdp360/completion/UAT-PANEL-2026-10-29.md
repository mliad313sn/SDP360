# External UAT panel — final review against the design purpose, 2026-10-29

Four independent seats reviewed the consolidation branch in a real browser (test PostgreSQL, French and
English, 1280 px and 390 px) and against the design documents (`docs/sdp360/01…08`, `completion/`):

| Seat | Who it stands for |
|---|---|
| PMO + business analyst | portfolio and governance director, requirements traceability (20 requirements traced) |
| ITSM | ITIL 4 process owner and service desk manager |
| Enterprise architect | TOGAF review of architecture, operability, deployment and maintenance |
| Users, adoption, heads of department | site IT coordinator, plant head of department, finance head, HR head, group service manager, executive |

Status: **FIXED** (code + test on this branch) · **OPEN — code** (next work item) · **HUMAN GATE** (a person or
the committee decides; nothing is built until then). Evidence (scripts, logs, screenshots) stays in the
reviewers' work folders; the reproducible proof of each fix is its test.
