STRATEGIC OUTCOME
Keep and improve SDP360: do not rebuild it, split it into microservices without a demonstrated need, or replace ServiceDesk Plus. Make SDP360 the trusted management layer that links business priorities, department meetings, process incubations, preventive inspections, ITSM incidents/requests/changes, infrastructure health, business commitments, delivery, risks, evidence, verification, benefits and executive decisions. Translate IT delivery into business continuity, productive capacity, risk reduction, safety-related control assurance, user experience, expenditure and investment decisions when the source data supports such conclusions.

APPROVED PRODUCT BOUNDARIES — OVERRIDE EARLIER PROPOSALS
- SDP360 is the authoritative system of record for business commitments, initiatives, projects, programmes, portfolios, milestones, dependencies, resource and capacity plans, approved IT budget assumptions, internal approval decisions, status, risks, actions, benefits and evidence. Reuse and extend the repository's native portfolio/governance/tracker modules. Do not implement external project/portfolio management connectors or synchronize master project state from Planner, Project, DevOps or other planning tools.
- OpManager is the preferred single infrastructure observability source. Integrate only the OpManager device/service availability, alarm, fault, performance and link/capacity fields actually exposed by the deployed OpManager instance. Do not infer inventory, virtual platform metrics, link SLA, security findings or restore evidence that the deployment does not monitor. When coverage is absent, show NOT MONITORED/NO DATA, not a fabricated zero or healthy status.
- Explicitly exclude direct source connectors for Pronto ERP, OT/HSE, ISP systems or contract feeds, Nutanix, enterprise backup applications and Microsoft Defender. Do not bring them back as optional or conditional integrations. An existing direct infrastructure connector (such as Meraki) may remain dormant for backward compatibility, but new functionality and executive monitoring must prefer OpManager and avoid duplicate records/alerts.
- Preserve business-workflow integrations where relevant and supported: ServiceDesk Plus/Zoho for ITSM, Entra ID for SSO, Teams/SMTP for notifications, SharePoint for reviewed publication, Power BI/OData for consumption, optional Microsoft 365 calendar for approved routine scheduling, and the existing minimal 4MyPeople lookup if useful. Backups and recovery tests required for SDP360's own safety remain non-negotiable OPERATIONS controls, not business-data integrations.

The operating value chain is:
LISTEN (department meeting) → DISCOVER (incubation/process study) → PREVENT (inspection/monitoring) → COMMIT (decision, accountable owner, due date) → EXECUTE (work and service/project linkage) → VERIFY (evidence and independent business/control sign-off) → REALISE VALUE (benefit observation and sustainability review).

MANDATORY BUSINESS RULE
ONE canonical business commitment identity. ONE approved taxonomy. ONE accountable owner. ONE agreed due date or approved exception. ONE traceable evidence set. ONE independent closure decision. Maintain many-to-many source links. A closed SDP ticket, a completed technical task, a held meeting or a published presentation MUST NOT automatically count as a verified business outcome.

DELIVERABLE DOMAIN 1 — CANONICAL BUSINESS SIGNALS AND COMMITMENTS [P0]
Build the governed cross-source layer over existing modules rather than another independent task list.
- Define distinct entity kinds: business need, observation, inspection finding, control weakness, operational risk, issue, corrective action, ITSM request/incident/problem/change, project/deliverable, improvement opportunity, decision and measured benefit.
- Capture origin system, source entity ID, site, business department, process, impact dimension, time, detector, sponsor and source URL/record pointer. Preserve source provenance; distinguish manual and integrated records.
- Model many-to-many signal ↔ commitment ↔ source work item ↔ evidence ↔ benefit relations with unique natural keys and reversible, authorized de-duplication/merge. Cross-site commitments require explicit multi-site scoping and authorization.
- Implement a canonical commitment state machine: captured → triaged → approved/committed → in_progress → awaiting_verification → verified_closed. Also support blocked, deferred, cancelled_with_reason, rejected_for_rework, reopened and accepted_risk_exception with explicit allowed transitions and actor privileges.
- Distinguish technical completion timestamp from independent business/control acceptance timestamp. No source-system closed status may silently cause verified_closed.
- Require one accountable owner, execution owner(s), sponsor/control reviewer, due date or approved exception, priority, treatment/plan for serious risk, and retained event history; allow precise role delegation/absence handling.
- Enforce optimistic concurrency, idempotent create/sync operations, append-only audit events, site/department scoping and server-side validation. Make due-date changes explainable and auditable; retain baseline due dates.
- On high/critical findings, require owner, severity justification, time-bound treatment, due date or approved authorized exception. Trigger escalations for unowned, undated, overdue and failed controls. Never mask these risks behind a composite KPI score.
- Support structured evidence with evidence type, actor, timestamp, checksum, allowed file types, malware/size checking, site scope, retention class and immutable reference/version. Avoid inline large base64 uploads and unrestricted public links.
- Business/control reviewers can accept, reject for rework, or acknowledge controlled residual risk only within approved authority. Reopening preserves previous verification and audit history.
- Preserve existing Site Actions Register as a linked consolidated view; do not erase original action records. Provide a migration reconciliation and duplicate-impact report before enabling canonical write paths.

Suggested tables, subject to existing-schema inspection: business_signals, commitments, commitment_source_links, commitment_events, evidence_items, closure_verifications, benefit_claims, benefit_observations, routine_schedules, risk_escalations and site_department_process_registry. Use proper foreign keys, uniqueness, versioning, indexes and archival policies; adapt names to existing conventions. Do not create a duplicate table if the live domain already provides its full semantics.
