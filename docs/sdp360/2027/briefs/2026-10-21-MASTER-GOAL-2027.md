# F. ACCEPTANCE DEMONSTRATIONS REQUIRED FOR SIGN-OFF

1. Department Head raises a need in an approved site meeting; a unique commitment links to it; IT completes an SDP ticket; business reviewer rejects inadequate evidence; the commitment is reopened, repaired and independently verified; reports show the correct history.
2. High/critical inspection finding without owner/date triggers server-side rejection or an authorized exception; the Friday pack shows escalation, and only the control owner can verify closure.
3. Duplicate meeting and inspection data are detected as reviewable candidates; historical source records remain intact and site totals reconcile against source IDs.
4. Offline technician captures inspection evidence on constrained/mobile connection; sync is idempotent, resumed uploads match checksums, replay conflicts are visible and sensitive cached records are removed on logout.
5. Role and scope boundaries hold: the Department Head sees only owned/authorized records, a different site IT lead cannot fetch cross-site evidence, and a Finance reviewer has no global admin privileges.
6. Exactly the same versioned KPI produces the same figure, eligibility count and explanatory source drilldown in site dashboard, executive cockpit, Power BI feed, monthly PPTX and CSV; native portfolio milestone, risk and approval values reconcile without a third-party PM system.
7. A source connector fails or becomes stale; the data-health pack alerts operators, dependent reports are blocked or prominently qualified, and no false zero/green reaches executives.
8. A generated monthly value report remains a reviewed draft until explicit approval; its released snapshot is immutable, scoped and auditable; revocation of a share link works.
9. A business incubation produces a hypothesis/option/validated sponsor, a native SDP360 portfolio intake decision, approved project plan, accountable milestones/dependencies and verified delivery acceptance; claimed monetary benefit remains forecast until Finance validates supported actuals.
10. Restore, rollback, dependency/security checks and field/UAT evidence are recorded before any production rollout.
