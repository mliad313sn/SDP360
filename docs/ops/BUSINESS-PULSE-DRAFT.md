# Business relationship pulse — DRAFT instrument (catalogue R14, audit R-05)

**Status: PROPOSAL ONLY.** Nothing here is approved. No survey has been sent and no instrument has been created in
any tool (Zoho Survey, Forms or the dashboard). The questions, the scale, the periodicity, the audience, the
anonymity rule and the confidentiality of the answers are decisions for the business-relationship owner and the CIO
(catalogue decision D-13). Every line below is a proposal.

## 1. Purpose (proposal)

- Proposal: measure, once a quarter, how site leaders and function heads perceive IT as a business partner — a
  question the helpdesk CSAT survey cannot answer (CSAT is per ticket and comes from requesters).
- Proposal: feed the answers into the existing commitment register (R03) so that a weak score becomes a tracked
  action, not a slide.

## 2. Audience (proposal)

- Proposal: site general managers, department heads met in the departmental meetings (PV), function heads at group
  level, and the CIO's direct reports. Never technicians answering about themselves.
- Proposal: one response per person per quarter.

## 3. Questions (proposal)

Scale proposal for Q1–Q8: 1 = strongly disagree … 5 = strongly agree, plus "not applicable" (stored as no answer,
never as 0).

| # | Theme | Proposed question |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Relationship | Proposal: "I know who my IT contact is and can reach them when I need to." |
| Q2 | Relationship | Proposal: "IT understands the priorities of my site / function." |
| Q3 | Responsiveness | Proposal: "When I raise a need, IT acknowledges it within an acceptable time." |
| Q4 | Responsiveness | Proposal: "Urgent operational problems are handled with the urgency they need." |
| Q5 | Value | Proposal: "The IT services I use help my team reach its objectives." |
| Q6 | Value | Proposal: "IT proposes improvements, not only fixes." |
| Q7 | Commitments kept | Proposal: "What IT committed to in our last meeting was delivered or its delay explained." |
| Q8 | Commitments kept | Proposal: "I am told when a commitment is closed and I can check the result." |
| Q9 | Overall | Proposal: "Overall, how satisfied are you with IT as a partner this quarter?" (1–5) |
| Q10 | Open | Proposal: "What is the one thing IT should do differently next quarter?" (free text) |

## 4. Anonymity options (to decide)

- Option A (proposal): anonymous — no name stored; only the site and the role family are kept. Lowest bias; a weak
  answer cannot be followed up with the person.
- Option B (proposal): confidential — the name is stored but visible only to the business-relationship owner;
  results are shown aggregated (minimum group of 3 answers per site before a site figure is shown).
- Option C (proposal): named — answers attributed. Easiest follow-up; highest bias.
- Whatever option is chosen, free text (Q10) is never published verbatim outside the owner's view without consent.

## 5. How results would feed commitments (proposal)

- Proposal: an average below 3 on a theme for a site raises ONE commitment of kind `improvement` on that site,
  owner = the site IT lead, reviewer = the business-relationship owner (four-eyes rule of the register applies).
- Proposal: Q7/Q8 are compared with the commitment register's own figures (verified on time); a gap between
  perception and register is itself an agenda item for the Friday governance review.
- Proposal: a figure with fewer than 3 answers is shown as UNKNOWN, never as a score.
- Proposal: nothing is computed into any site score until the KPI committee adopts a formula.

## 6. Periodicity and timing (to decide)

- Proposal: quarterly, opened for two weeks after the quarter's last monthly report, reminders in-app only.

## 7. Left for people

- Approve or change the questions, scale and audience (business-relationship owner, CIO).
- Choose the anonymity option and the confidentiality rule (with the data-protection contact).
- Decide the tool (an existing survey tool or a dashboard form) — no new external connector is assumed here.
- Decide whether and how results enter commitments and any score (KPI committee).
