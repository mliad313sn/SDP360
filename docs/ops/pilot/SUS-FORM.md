## Scoring (done by the facilitator, not the participant)

- Odd items (1, 3, 5, 7, 9): contribution = answer − 1.
- Even items (2, 4, 6, 8, 10): contribution = 5 − answer.
- SUS score = sum of the 10 contributions × 2.5 (range 0–100).
- A questionnaire with an unanswered item is recorded as **incomplete** — no score is computed for it, and it
  is not counted as 0.
- Report the median and the number of questionnaires per role. No target score is set without a baseline and
  a sponsor decision.
