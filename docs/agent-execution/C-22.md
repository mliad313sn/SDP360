## Deviations
- Emergency grants are approved by the granter alone (R7 says "a super_admin"); the second human is the post-reviewer, not a
  pre-approver (check-IAM G49 sketch said "second approver"; R7 wins).
- Hour-precision end: access_grants keep date validity; `access_emergencies.expires_at` drives the 10-minute job
  (and every emergency list read), so a role can outlive its hour by ≤ 10 minutes. `access_grant_expiry_job` is not required.
- Qualifications are not "checked at grant time" — the grant is allowed, the C-19 gate still refuses the capability, and the
  grant response lists `qualification_unmet`.
- A break-glass via Admin ▸ Users with the switch on now ends after 24 h (it used to be permanent) — that is the intended change.
- Tests written alongside the code, not strictly before.
